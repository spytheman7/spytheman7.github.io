Privacy Policy

AI Shorts Studio · Effective date: November 10, 2026

AI Shorts Studio ("the App") is a personal content-production tool operated by an individual creator to produce short-form videos and publish them to the operator's own social media accounts, including TikTok. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information we collect

  • TikTok account information you authorize through TikTok Login Kit: open ID, display name, avatar, username, follower and video counts (scopes: user.info.basic, user.info.profile, user.info.stats).
  • OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) issued by TikTok so the App can upload videos you choose to publish (scopes: video.upload, video.publish).
  • Content you create in the App, such as videos, captions and hashtags you choose to upload.

The App does not collect information about other TikTok users, does not read your messages, and does not access your contacts.

2. How we use information

  • To connect your TikTok account and show which account is connected.
  • To upload videos to your TikTok account (to your drafts/inbox or as a post) only when you request it.
  • To check upload status and show basic channel statistics to you.

3. Storage and security

Information is stored only in the App's local database on the operator's own computer. It is not stored on third-party servers by the App, and access to the App is restricted to the operator.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok's official API to perform the actions you request.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens and profile data are kept while your account is connected. You can disconnect your account in the App at any time, which deletes the stored tokens and profile data. You can also revoke access from TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and does not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
